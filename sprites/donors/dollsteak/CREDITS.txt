THE CONVERSION, AND WHY IT IS SAFE
----------------------------------
Not a quantisation. The source measures **exactly 16 distinct RGB values, every
pixel alpha=255**, so a 16-entry indexed palette is exact, not lossy. The conversion
ASSERTS `len(distinct_opaque_colours) <= 16` and refuses rather than guessing — the
same guard `import_donor_front_pics.to_indexed()` applies to rogue/lysandre_front.png,
and it exists because silently dropping colours to fit 4bpp yields plausible-looking
corrupt art that is only ever caught by eye.

Three properties were then verified on the written file, not assumed:
  * pixel-for-pixel identical to the source after converting back to RGB
    (0 differing pixels of 4096)
  * palette written with EXACTLY 16 entries and index range 0..15 — deliberately
    not left at PIL's default 256-entry padding, which is the SPRITE_PLAN section 5
    trap (a PNG declaring 256 entries while using 16 colours; `to_4bpp`'s `& 0xF`
    mangles it silently when the indices are out of range, and passes only by luck
    when they are not)
  * the source's corner pixel colour (248, 208, 224) is forced to **index 0**, the
    background slot pokeemerald front pics require
