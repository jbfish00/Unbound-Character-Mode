WHAT WAS DONE TO THE ART
------------------------
The sheet is ten chibi professors in a row. Nine are identified from the
deviation's own tags and staged; the tenth (purple-haired) is not tagged and is
NOT staged -- names are not guessed.

Each figure was cut out at its transparent column boundaries, trimmed to its
opaque rows, and placed unscaled on a 64x64 trainer-pic canvas (centred, feet
4 px above the bottom), with palette index 0 transparent. Order on the sheet:
oak, elm, birch, rowan, juniper, sycamore, kukui, magnolia, sonia, (untagged).

Two figures use more than the 15 opaque colours a GBA sprite allows; the
closest colour pairs were merged, least-used into most-used:
  magnolia: (192,152,72) -> (176,136,88), 44 px
  sonia:    (116,61,46) -> (117,62,47), 8 px; (120,64,64) -> (117,62,47), 52 px
Every other figure is pixel-identical to the original.

These are overworld-style chibi figures, not battle front pics; they fill the
front-pic slot for professors who had no art at all.
