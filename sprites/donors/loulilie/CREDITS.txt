RETRIEVAL METHOD (worth keeping — this is the part that bites)
--------------------------------------------------------------
DeviantArt full-size image URLs require the ?token= query string; bare wixmp
URLs return a 12-byte "Unauthorized". r.jina.ai does NOT work on DeviantArt, so
the deviation pages were fetched directly with curl and a browser User-Agent,
and the tokened URL pulled out of the markup (src/full_urls.json).

Important: the markup carries several tokened variants. Only the BARE
  .../<uuid>.png?token=...
URL is the lossless original. The /v1/fill/... and /v1/fit/... variants are
resized and JPEG-recompressed (q_80 / q_70) and are useless for pixel art. The
bare variant is the shortest of the tokened URLs, which is how it was selected.
Tokens are time-limited — they will not still work later; re-fetch the page.
