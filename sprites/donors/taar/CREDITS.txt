ATTRIBUTION REQUIREMENT
-----------------------
Free to use and edit, BUT credit to the original creator is required. TAAR is organised as
  <asset type>/<creator or project>/...
so the second path element of every file under files/ IS the attribution. Per-author
README/credit files were harvested alongside the art and are the authoritative record where a
folder aggregates work by more than one person (74 such files captured; see manifest.json
"aux_files"). Before shipping any sprite, read the README.md in its author folder -- some
folders re-credit an upstream creator (e.g. the "Delta231s Collaborative HGSS Resource" tree
credits Delta231, M.vit, Mimi, aveontrainer, etique, robloxmaster342, ulithium_dragon
separately per subfolder).

Author folders present in the harvested trees (second path element) -- credit these:
  see manifest.json; every file's `path` begins with "<tree>/<author>/".
