CONTENT
-------
64x64 indexed trainer front sprites and 64x256/64x320 back sprites in Platinum style.
Covers Cynthia, Cyrus, Dawn, Lucas, Barry, Bertha, Lucian, Volkner, Candice, Maylene,
Fantina, Roark, Byron, Crasher Wake and Mars/Jupiter/Saturn, plus the inherited Hoenn cast.
All 196 images passed verification with no flags.
