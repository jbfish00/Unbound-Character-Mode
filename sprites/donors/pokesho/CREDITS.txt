LICENCE (quoted verbatim from the archived pages)
-------------------------------------------------
Trainer gallery header (dot_battle.html):

    「GBA風トレーナードット絵を展示しています。フリー素材になります。」

    "GBA-style trainer pixel art is on display here. These are free materials."

  ...and immediately below it, the same page adds:

    「こちらではGBA版ポケモンの仕様にあわせたトレーナーのグラフィックを展示しています。
      すべて64×64サイズ。透明色合わせて最大16色です。素材としての使用も可能です。」

    "Here we display trainer graphics matched to the specifications of the GBA
     Pokemon games. All are 64x64 in size. Maximum 16 colours including the
     transparent colour. Use as material is also permitted."

Field gallery header (dot_field.html):

    「GBA風フィールドドット絵を配布しています。」
    「こちらでは『ファイアレッド・リーフグリーン版』の仕様にあわせた
      フィールドグラフィックを配布しています。すべて縦22・横16ピクセルです。」

    "GBA-style field pixel art is distributed here."
    "Here we distribute field graphics matched to the specifications of
     FireRed / LeafGreen. All are 22 pixels tall by 16 pixels wide."

Site FAQ (standard/example.html), under よくあるしつもん / "Frequently asked questions":

    「素材もらってもいいですか！？」
    「どうぞどうぞ！！」

    "May I take the materials!?"
    "Please, go right ahead!!"

The same FAQ also states 「ポケモンの関係者ですか！？」→「違います！！」
("Are you affiliated with Pokemon?" — "No!!"), i.e. this is unofficial fan art.

CONTENTS
--------
battle/   68 files — 64x64 trainer battle sprites ("front" pics).
                     ALL 68 verified at exactly 64x64, mode P, single frame.
field/   309 files — FRLG-spec field/overworld sprites.
                     306 verified at exactly 16x22; 3 are slightly undersized
                     (see manifest.json).
                     IMPORTANT: these are a SINGLE front-facing frame each, NOT
                     a 9-frame walk cycle. They are marked "partial" in
                     manifest.json and need the remaining frames authored before
                     they can be used as a complete FireRed NPC sheet.
zips/               the two original .zip downloads, kept as-received (the
                    redundant raw unzip directories were removed; re-extract
                    from these if you need a pristine copy).

Note: the zips contain slightly more than the 2018 gallery index pages listed
(309 field files vs 298 indexed; 68 battle vs 68 indexed). Field entries with no
section in the index are recorded as "unlisted-in-2018-index" in manifest.json.
