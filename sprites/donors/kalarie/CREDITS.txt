TECHNICAL CAVEAT (from the project brief, carry this into any repo README)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
These sheets do not all fit FireRed's default overworld palettes. Using them as
a set requires Navenatox's Dynamic Overworld Palettes patch.

IDENTIFICATION CAVEAT
---------------------
The sprites are UNLABELLED at source: the thread posts them as a bare grid with
no captions, the post's alt text is generic, and the imgur pages carry no
titles. Character names in manifest.json are therefore VISUAL identifications
made from the sprites themselves, and each entry records a confidence level:

  high    (9)  Misty(062) Brock(063) James(064) Jessie(065) Meowth(066)
               Butch(067) Cassidy(068) Officer Jenny(077) Nurse Joy(078)
  medium  (3)  Officer Jenny variant(060) A.J.(105) Professor Oak(115)
  low     (3)  Professor Ivy(079) Ivy's assistant(080) scientist NPC(098)
  none   (54)  recorded as "unidentified anime NPC (thread image #N)"

The 54 unidentified sheets are real, verified, usable art — only their character
attribution is open. Do not treat the low-confidence three as settled.
