# Character Mode — Final-Evolution Rosters (Pokémon Unbound v2.1.1.1)

Every playable character and the **final evolutions** their complete roster resolves to, in **National Pokédex order**. Rosters were researched from Bulbapedia (union of all games, remakes, rematches, and anime) and cross-checked where possible. Regional/cosmetic forms show as their base species. Off-roster Pokémon are routed to your PC.

> Species above National Dex #905 are omitted (not present in this game).

**193 characters.** Sprite version: `ROSTERS_SPRITES.md`.

GENERATED by `tools/character_mode/emit_roster_docs.py` from `rosters.bin`, the same injected roster blob the enforcement shim walks — do not hand-edit, regenerate.

### Coverage

- **193 characters**, **3230 final-evolution rows**; every row is derived from `rosters.bin`, the injected blob the enforcement shim walks, so nothing here is promised that the ROM refuses.
- **3135 of 3230 rows (97%) carry a Source.** A blank Source means the provenance is not recorded, not that the entry is unverified — the 2026-07-25 adversarial audit judged every roster in this file.
- **15 characters are hidden from the menu** and are therefore not listed here: Burnet, Drasna, Drew, Gordie, Grant, Laventon, Magnolia, Melony, Olympia, Opal, Sonia, Sophocles, Tracey, Viola, Wulfric. Each has fewer than six fully-evolved Pokémon obtainable in this game's dex, with no legendary to exempt it, so the character-select screen refuses the number. They keep their table slots — character ids are save data, so the remaining numbers are unchanged and simply skip these.
